About Us

An overview of FBLA, our mission, and the Oakdale chapter's history.

Our Organization

What is FBLA?

Future Business Leaders of America (FBLA) is the largest career and technical student organization in the world focused solely on business. Established in 1942, FBLA prepares students for careers in business through leadership development, competitive events, and community service, connecting classroom learning to real-world experience.

Mission

Our Mission

FBLA inspires and prepares students to become community-minded business leaders in a global society through relevant career preparation and leadership experiences.

Meet

When & Where We Meet

We meet every Club Day in Room B208 at Oakdale High School. Email us at fblaoakdale@gmail.com to get started.

History

Our History

The Oakdale High School FBLA chapter was founded in 2024 by Ajaypranav Vinod. The chapter is advised by Brian Ranallo and competes at Maryland's District, State, and National conferences each year. In that time, we've sent 35 members to States and 10 members to Nationals!

What You'll Gain

What inspires students to join Oakdale FBLA.

Leadership Skills

Competitive Events

Networking & Community

Meet like-minded peers, connect with local business professionals, and give back through service projects.
